## Шаблон (общ за всички)

**Subject:** DRUM — parcels in cars already on the road · €150K seed · Bulgaria

Здравейте,

[ПЕРСОНАЛИЗАЦИЯ — виж по-долу]

Every day hundreds of thousands of cars cross Bulgaria's main corridors with
empty trunks, while sending a 2 kg parcel costs €6+ and takes 1–3 days. DRUM
uses the transport that already happens instead of paying for a new trip.

How it works: the sender posts a request in a Telegram bot → funds are locked
in Stripe escrow → pickup and delivery are confirmed by QR → the payment
splits automatically (carrier 80%, DRUM retains €0.56 per delivery = 7.2%) →
Trust Score reputation + a per-delivery carbon ledger under GHG Protocol
Scope 3 (25.38 kg CO₂e saved per Sofia↔Plovdiv run).

Status, honestly: the platform is built and fully tested — 142/142 tests,
green CI, the whole escrow → QR → payout → CO₂ flow works end to end. Real
users: zero. That is exactly what this round is for.

We are raising **€150K for 15% (€1M post-money)** in our Bulgarian EOOD
(DRUM Tech), on standard seed terms. First 90 days: a paid validation on the
Sofia↔Plovdiv corridor against fixed bars — 100+ active users, 500+
deliveries, ≥35% repeat rate. Then: 3 corridors, B2B batch engine, team of 3.

Use of funds: €40K pilot · €45K product · €50K growth · €15K legal & buffer.

Would you be open to a 20-minute call? One-pager attached.

Best regards,
Димитър Христов (Dimitri Hristov)
Founder, DRUM
github.com/hristovdimitri2-hub/drum-platform
