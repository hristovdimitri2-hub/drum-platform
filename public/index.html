🌿 ESG by design, not as an afterthought

Every DRUM delivery removes a dedicated courier trip. The calculation follows GHG Protocol Scope 3, Category 4 with an open formula: savings = (baseline − marginal) × km = 0.175 × km. Sofia–Plovdiv (145 km): 25.38 kg CO₂e saved per delivery — full methodology and audit trail, grant-application ready.

Trust protocol — in short

Escrow

Funds frozen at request (Stripe auth-only), released only on confirmed delivery (QR).

Trust Score 0–100

Event-sourced reputation: +5 carrier (1/day, cap +100), +3 sender; penalties on failures and disputes; 2 lost disputes in 90 days → ban.

Tiers

Banned <31 · Limited 31–49 (≤€20) · Standard 50–69 (≤€200) · Verified 70–89 (≤€500) · Premium 90+ (≤€1000, B2B API).

Strict Agent Model

The platform NEVER assigns a carrier — it shows options, the user chooses (Uber Spain C-434/15).
